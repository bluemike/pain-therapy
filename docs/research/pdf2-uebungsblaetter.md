# PDF 2: "Sammelmappe 27 Übungsblätter .pdf"

Source: OneDrive-AyantaAG/Ayanta Home - Dokumente/pain-therapy/. The file has 27 pages, was made with Adobe and has a text layer. It holds 27 patient exercise handouts, one exercise per page, with photos and numbered steps. The handouts address the patient as "du".

## Page layout (the same on every page)
- **Title:** code and name, for example "1A Kiefer-Übung".
- **Safety note:** practise slowly and consciously. The intensity (pain, effort, reluctance) must never reach 10. Questions go to the L&B doctor or therapist.
- **Prescription fields** filled in by the therapist:
  - Häufigkeit pro Woche (times per week)
  - täglich (times per day)
  - Intensität (intensity)
  - Schritte (steps)
- **Faszien-Rollmassage:** which areas to roll with which tool (Mini-Kugel or Medi-Kugel, Mini-Rolle or Medi-Rolle).
- **Engpassdehnung:** the stretch, in 3 steps (see below).
- **Varianten:** easier or harder versions. Many use L&B tools:
  - Schlaufe (strap)
  - Knieretter-Keil (wedge)
  - Nackenretter
  - Rückenretter
  - Schulterretter
  - ISG-Ischias-Retter
  - Kieferretter
- **Footer:** an ad for the L&B app (3 months free) with a QR code.

## The 3-step pattern (the same for every exercise)
1. Move into the stretch and increase it for 30 s.
2. Tense against resistance for 7 s without moving, release, then stretch further for 3 s. Do this 3 times (30 s in total). Then repeat the same thing faster for 30 s, about 8 to 12 cycles.
3. Hold the end position actively, with your own strength, for 30 s.

Most exercises are described for the right side. Some apply to the affected side only, others to both sides. Back complaints, for example, mean both sides.

## Exercise catalogue
### Group 1: head and neck
- **1A Kiefer (jaw):** open the mouth wide, with the web of the thumb on the chin.
- **1B Kiefergelenk (jaw joint):** pull the jaw to the side without turning the head, on both sides.
- **1C Augen (eyes):** look in one direction with fingertips resting lightly on the eyelid, in all 4 directions.
  - Take contact lenses out first.
  - Never touch the eyeball.
- **1D Nacken (neck):** turn the head 45° and pull it diagonally towards the opposite knee.
- **1E Halswirbelsäule (cervical spine):** roll the head in and pull the chin towards the larynx.
- **1F Rotation:** shoulder against the wall, rotate the trunk and head.

### Group 2: trunk and hip
- **2A Wirbelsäule (spine):** sit with the soles together and pull the trunk forward and the head down.
- **2B Brust 10° (chest):** lie on the stomach with the arm 10° above shoulder level and rotate the trunk away.
- **2C Atem (breathing):**
  - Breathe out fully and drop forward.
  - "Suck" against a closed nose and mouth.
  - Finish with 12 slow belly breaths.
- **2D Bauch (abdomen):** on all fours, let the pelvis sag, groin first, with the navel drawn in.
- **2E Gesäß (buttocks):** shin across the other leg, hollow back.
- **2F Vordere Oberschenkel (front thigh):** lie on the stomach with a wedge under the knee and pull the heel towards the buttock with a strap.

### Group 3: shoulder and arm
- **3A Schulter-Rücken (shoulder, back):** strap around the elbows, hands on the floor, sternum towards the knees.
- **3B Obere Brust 45° (upper chest):** like 2B, with the arm at 45°.
- **3C Handbeuger (wrist flexors):** hand turned outward on a table, shoulder pulled back.
- **3D Handstrecker (wrist extensors):** make a fist with the thumb inside and bend the wrist against the table.
- **3E Arm-Innendreher (internal rotators):** strap above the elbow, rotate outward against the wall.
- **3F Hintere Schulter (back of shoulder):** lie on the side and roll onto the arm.
- **3G Armstrecker (triceps):** lie on the stomach, arm overhead on a wedge, hand towards the shoulder.
- **3H Arm-Schulter (arm, shoulder):** straight arms behind on the floor or a table, chest open, then "walk" the hands back.

### Group 4: leg
- **4A Äußere Bein-Rumpf (outer leg, trunk):** standing, one foot crossed behind in a strap, bend the trunk to the side.
- **4B Hintere Bein (back of leg):** leg straight, strap around the forefoot, trunk forward with a hollow back.
- **4C Waden (calf):** forefoot on the Knieretter wedge, knee straight, pelvis forward.
- **4D Tiefe Waden (deep calf):** like 4C with the knee slightly bent, which targets the Achilles tendon.
- **4E Schienbein-Zehenstrecker (shin, toe extensors):** pull the forefoot down and curl the toes.
- **4F Zehenbeuger (toe flexors):** pull the forefoot and toes up.
- **4G Innere Oberschenkel (inner thigh):** lean on a table and walk the feet apart.

The list is complete for codes 1A–1F, 2A–2F, 3A–3H and 4A–4G. These are the same codes the treatment forms in PDF 1 use under "Engpassdehnungen".

## Data-model notes
- **Exercise:** code, group, name, steps, rolling instructions (area and tool), variants, which side, and a handout PDF or images.
- **Prescription per patient and exercise:** times per week, times per day, intensity, and which steps.
- **Patient log:** whether the exercise was done, the intensity (0–9), and the difficulty (leicht/mittel/schwer/unmöglich, i.e. easy, medium, hard, impossible, as on the treatment form).
