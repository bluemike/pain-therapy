# PDF 1: "Sammelmappe 12 Behandlungsbögen_Liebscher & Bracht.pdf"

Source: OneDrive-AyantaAG/Ayanta Home - Dokumente/pain-therapy/. The file has 12 pages, was made in Canva and has a text layer. It holds 12 therapist documentation forms titled "Dokumentation L&B Schmerztherapie", one per body region.

## Form layout (the same on every sheet)
- **Header:** Diagnose (diagnosis); Name; Datum (date); Behandlung Nr. (treatment number); Schmerz ___ % (pain in percent); Vergleich (comparison, free text).
- **Osteopressur:** one row per pressure point with these columns: point ID (for example 1.1.1); muscle and attachment, in Latin with German in brackets; treated (left/right checkboxes); sensitivity (niedrig/mittel/stark/extrem, i.e. low, medium, strong, extreme).
  - The point names are colour-coded in black, orange and orange bold. The sheet has no legend for this. The colours most likely show how relevant a point is to the region, with orange bold as the main points.
- **Restschmerz nach Behandlung** (residual pain after treatment): a scale from 0 to 100% in steps of 10.
- **Engpassdehnungen** (stretches): one row per exercise code (for example 1A). Each row has a "verordnet" (prescribed) checkbox, the difficulty ("Übung fällt": leicht/mittel/schwer/unmöglich, i.e. easy, medium, hard, impossible) and free-text variants.
- **Faszien-Rollmassage** (fascia rolling): 6 slots, each pairing an area (Bereich) with a tool (Kugel/Rolle, i.e. ball or roller), plus free-text lines. Tool abbreviations:
  - Mini-Kugel = MiKu
  - Medi-Kugel = MeKu
  - Mini-Rolle = MiRo
  - Medi-Rolle = MeRo

## Data-model notes
- **Session:** belongs to a patient and a region sheet (1–12), with a date and a treatment number. It records pain in % before treatment and the residual pain after it (0–100%).
- **Per point:** treated on the left and/or right side, and sensitivity on 4 levels.
- **Per exercise:** prescribed or not, difficulty on 4 levels, and free-text variants.
- **Rolling:** up to 6 pairs of area and tool.
- **Sharing:** regions share points and exercises, so the catalogue is many-to-many.
